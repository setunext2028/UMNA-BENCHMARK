Other abort paths (any of these -> ABSTAIN):
   - no admissible graph
   - wing failure with no fallback
   - budget exhausted mid-plan
   - conflicting evidence above threshold

NEVER: resolve an unresolved state by picking the higher score.
Abstention is counted, capped per task family; abstaining your way to
good numbers is a failure mode of the whole system.
